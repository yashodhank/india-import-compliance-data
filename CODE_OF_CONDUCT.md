# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, as adopted in the maintainer's other open-source repositories.

## Our standards

Be respectful and constructive. Argue from the official source: a correction is judged by the page or Gazette it points to, not by who sent it.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, sexualised language, publishing someone's private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@yashodhank](https://github.com/yashodhank)) may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block a user from the repository. Report a problem privately through **Security > Report a vulnerability** on the repository page; reports are handled in confidence.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
